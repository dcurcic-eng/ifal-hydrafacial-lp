WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

What to expect during your HydraFacial.

Your appointment begins with a brief skin assessment and a conversation with your aesthetician about your concerns and goals. From there, your treatment proceeds through the three HydraFacial steps, cleanse and peel, extract and hydrate, and fuse and protect, followed by LED light therapy.

The treatment is comfortable, relaxing, and most clients describe it as feeling like a cool paintbrush gently moving across the skin. The full appointment takes about 60 minutes, and you can return to your normal activities immediately afterward, including makeup, if desired.
